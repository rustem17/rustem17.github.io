I study how to understand and oversee AI systems: what motivates their behavior, how they generalize, and when we can trust our evaluations. I'm a Research Scholar at MATS, working with Prof. Shi Feng.

My current work asks how realistic model organisms of misalignment are, and where existing alignment and oversight methods may fail under recursive self-improvement (RSI). I'm interested in how these methods, and the assumptions behind them, need to change as models improve themselves. I'm also interested in randomizing the oversight counterparts an untrusted model interacts with, to make it harder to exploit predictable oversight.

I'm interested in reinforcement learning: curricula and reward design that provide rich feedback while leaving room for models to discover solutions and develop capabilities themselves. I'm also interested in using RL to transfer capabilities from tasks with crisp, verifiable objectives to more open-ended, fuzzy tasks.

at Palisade Research, I evaluated LLM agents in offensive security settings and studied their misbehavior.

I'm fascinated by how music takes shape: how simple ideas become deeply expressive through the way they develop, intertwine, and return. I'm drawn to the interplay of structure and feeling, and I'd love to build tools that help explore and compose music with that kind of depth.
